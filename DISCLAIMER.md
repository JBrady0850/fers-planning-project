## Accuracy and currency

Federal benefit rules change, and dollar figures change every year.

- Rules in this repository were verified in **September 2026**.
- Dollar figures are **2026 program year** values. Contribution limits, insurance premiums, IRMAA brackets, the retirement earnings test exempt amount, Medicare costs and cost of living adjustments all change annually.
- `docs/06-fers-rules-reference.md` names **ten items that could not be verified or where sources conflict**. Those are stated openly rather than resolved. Read that list.
- Two OPM Handbook chapters cited carry text that statute has since superseded. Both are flagged where they appear.
- Statute and regulation can change. Proposals affecting the FERS Special Retirement Supplement and the high-3 computation were introduced in 2025 and removed before enactment. They can return.

**Re-verify any rule at opm.gov, tsp.gov, ssa.gov, medicare.gov or irs.gov before it drives a filing decision.** Where this repository and a primary source disagree, the primary source governs and this repository is wrong.

## Irreversible elections

The following federal retirement elections are permanent or effectively permanent. Confirm each one with your agency benefits officer and a licensed fiduciary before you file:

- The survivor annuity election on your retirement application
- The FEGLI post retirement reduction election on SF 2818
- Cancelling rather than suspending FEHB as an annuitant
- Choosing deferred retirement over postponed retirement
- Paying or not paying a military service deposit before you separate
- Your Social Security claiming age
- Any TSP life annuity purchase

## Artificial intelligence produces errors

This system runs on a large language model. Language models make arithmetic errors, misread documents, and state incorrect things confidently. The master prompt is built to reduce that risk through source citation requirements, shown arithmetic, and an explicit definition of done, and those controls reduce the error rate. They do not eliminate it.

**Check the arithmetic on anything that drives a decision.** If a figure surprises you, ask for the source and the calculation. The prompt instructs the assistant to provide both.
