# OPEN ITEMS

Every unanswered question, each with the document that would close it and an estimate of how much the answer could move the plan. Ranked by impact, not by age. Reviewed at the end of every monthly cycle.

**Format.** One item per row. When an item closes, move it to the Closed section with the date and what the answer turned out to be. Nothing is deleted.

---

## OPEN

| # | Question | Document that closes it | Where to get it | Potential impact | Opened |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is my Service Computation Date for retirement purposes, and does it differ from my leave SCD? | Written confirmation from the benefits office | Agency benefits office, in writing | Moves every eligibility date and the years of service in the annuity formula. Highest impact item in the plan. | 2026-09-13 |
| 2 | Is there any federal service in my record for which retirement deductions were not withheld? | Written statement of non deduction service with dates and deposit amount | Agency benefits office, in writing | Unpaid pre-1989 deposit service counts for neither eligibility nor computation. Can push the unreduced date later by years. | 2026-09-13 |
| 3 | If I have military service, what is the current balance and deadline on my military service deposit? | Military deposit status letter | Agency benefits office or payroll provider | The deposit must be paid before separation. OPM cannot accept it afterward. | 2026-09-13 |
| 4 | Have I ever taken a refund of retirement contributions, and what redeposit would be required? | Refund record and redeposit statement | Agency benefits office or OPM | Unredeposited refunded service counts for eligibility but not for the annuity computation. | 2026-09-13 |
| 5 | On what date did my continuous FEHB enrollment begin? | FEHB enrollment history, SF 2809 and SF 2810 | Agency benefits office or eOPF | Decides whether each candidate separation date satisfies the five year rule. A failed test forfeits FEHB for life. | 2026-09-13 |
| 6 | On what dates did my FEGLI Basic and each Option begin? | FEGLI enrollment history, SF 2817 | Agency benefits office or eOPF | Same five year test, applied separately to Basic and each Option, and OPM cannot waive it. | 2026-09-13 |
| 7 | What does my Social Security benefit look like with future earnings set to zero? | SSA estimate run at $0 future earnings | ssa.gov, my Social Security Retirement Calculator | Feeds both the Social Security line and the FERS supplement computation. The statement figure overstates both. | 2026-09-13 |
| 8 | What is the current OPM retirement application processing timeline my agency expects? | Written answer from the benefits office | Agency benefits office | Sizes the cash bridge. Interim payments during adjudication are a fraction of the final annuity. | 2026-09-13 |

Items 1 through 8 are the standing opening set. Add to them as the interview surfaces gaps.
